chore: sweep UTF-8 mojibake + BOMs across 17 files — C9 full close

C9 originally scoped to requester.py and partner.py only. That was
incomplete. A project-wide scan (find all C3A2 byte sequences + all
UTF-8 BOMs) found 19 files with issues. The audit was right to flag
corruption but the audit list itself was too narrow.

Two classes of issue:

1. C3A2 mojibake (2 files, real corruption):
   - backend/src/mtc/routes/partner.py
   - backend/tests/test_requests.py
   
   Same pattern as C9's requester.py: intended UTF-8 characters
   (em-dashes, smart quotes, likely from copy-pasted comments) were
   mangled through a Windows-1252 round-trip and re-saved as UTF-8
   of the mangled characters. Fixed via iterative reverse:
   decode-UTF-8 -> encode-cp1252, applied 2-3 times until the C3A2
   marker no longer appears. Restored from original bytes if the
   iterative fix failed to converge (no partial writes).

2. UTF-8 BOM only (15 files, cosmetic):
   - CONTRIBUTING.md, ROADMAP.md, docs/roles.md
   - backend/alembic/versions/003_idempotency_keys.py,
     backend/alembic/versions/004_pledge_request_link.py
   - backend/src/mtc/routes/{admin,campaigns,fulfilment,helplines,
     opportunities,stats}.py
   - src/components/ErrorBoundary.tsx
   - src/components/Architecture/ArchitectureView.tsx
   - src/components/Requester/StatusTimeline.tsx
   - src/services/auth.ts
   
   BOMs are harmless to Python/TypeScript/Markdown parsers, but they
   are the signature of files that were written by PowerShell's
   Set-Content/Add-Content (which adds BOM) rather than
   [System.IO.File]::WriteAllText with UTF8Encoding($false). Fixed by
   stripping the leading EF BB BF bytes.

Deliberately skipped:
- .env (gitignored, user's local config)
- fix-history.ps1 (PowerShell scripts sometimes need BOM to correctly
  interpret non-ASCII; not worth the risk)
